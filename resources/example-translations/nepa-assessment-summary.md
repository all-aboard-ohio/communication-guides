# After: One-Page Summary for Community Meeting

## Proposed SR 42 Improvements: What You Need to Know

**Headline:** ODOT Plan Would Improve Traffic Flow on SR 42, But Could Increase Noise Near Oakwood Park

### Key Findings

1. **Traffic will flow better** — The project would improve traffic from a “D” rating (slow) to a “C” rating (moderate) by 2040.
2. **Noise may increase** — Homes within 200 feet of the new road could hear 3-5 decibels more traffic noise. ODOT is considering noise walls or berms to reduce the impact.
3. **A small wetland area will be affected** — About 0.8 acres of marshland will be replaced with new wetland elsewhere at double the size.

### What Happens Next

- ODOT will hold another public meeting before final approval.
- Residents can submit comments online or by mail until May 1, 2024.
- The final decision is expected in summer 2024.

### How to Get Involved

- **Attend the next meeting:** April 15, 2024, at 7 PM, Oakwood Community Center.
- **Submit comments:** Visit www.transportation.ohio.gov/sr42 or call (555) 123-4567.
- **Share this summary** with neighbors who may be affected.
