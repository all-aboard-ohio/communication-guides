# Original: NEPA Environmental Assessment (Excerpt)

**Project:** State Route 42 Corridor Improvement
**Agency:** Ohio Department of Transportation
**Date:** March 2024

## 3.2 Level of Service Analysis

Existing conditions indicate a Level of Service (LOS) D during peak hours at the intersection of SR 42 and Main Street. The proposed improvements are projected to achieve LOS C during the design year (2040). The analysis was conducted using the Highway Capacity Manual (HCM) methodology.

## 3.3 Environmental Consequences

### 3.3.1 Noise Impacts

Noise modeling predicts an increase of 3-5 dBA Leq(h) for receptors within 200 feet of the proposed alignment. Mitigation measures under 23 CFR 772 include noise barriers, berms, and buffer zones. A Section 4(f) evaluation is required due to potential impacts on Oakwood Park.

### 3.3.2 Wetland Impacts

Approximately 0.8 acres of palustrine emergent wetland will be permanently impacted. Mitigation will be achieved through the purchase of wetland credits from the Ohio Wetland Mitigation Bank at a 2:1 ratio.

## 3.4 Public Involvement

A public hearing was held on February 15, 2024, with 47 attendees. Comments were received from 23 individuals. Key concerns included traffic congestion during construction and property value impacts.
